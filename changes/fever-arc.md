## What shipped
Fever is one arc on the beat. In the gauge's last 20% a light band crosses the meter once a beat. The flash, banner, star streak and lit meter fire together on the beat after the judge's `feverStart`, not on the 12th hit. In the sustain the sparks' wisps and the stars warm to amber while the dust keeps the hand's color, and the streak swells on each beat. A combo break drains the edges and meter over 400 ms; a song ending in fever drains on results-landing's out (from frozen-clock-fades, #1333, on `landingT()`); without `?x=results-landing` it cuts at the results (`specs/decisions/frozen-clock-fades-fever.md`). Reduced motion cuts. Judging is untouched.
