## What shipped
The results take graph reads in five seconds. Bands come from the judge's live windows (an input, defaulting to core's 40/80/120). Each hand has a rolling median of 8 hits over its dimmed notes, with direct labels at the right end ("RH +40 ms late"). Velocity has its own strip, the x axis is in bars, MISS has its own row, and an offset past GOOD is a chevron at the edge, never a GOOD-looking dot. A crosshair readout card ("Bar 12 · beat 3 · +32 ms · GREAT · vel 74") follows the pointer, and arrow keys, Home and End step it. The graph draws in left to right over 600 ms the first time it's on screen. The read test: 3 of 3 after, against 3 of 3 before on the same planted takes, with before take 3's hand a stated guess.
