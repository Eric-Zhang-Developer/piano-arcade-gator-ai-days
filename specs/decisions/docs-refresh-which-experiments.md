doesn't name, at least the five in #1142. devpost.md named none. `changes/` also holds research spikes with no page
(hum-level, jam-mode, mascot, piano-midi, restyle, score-follow, snap-score, twinkle-13), which a judge can't open.

**Options.** (a) Every done note labeled experiment or spike. (b) Only what a judge can open: every Lab registry
entry (`web/src/game/x/`) and every spike with a page under `web/src/app/`.

**Choice.** (b): 14 lines, the five from #1142 plus Composer's Hands, Wet Paint, Speaking Piano, Pinned, Build Sonata,
Round, Hand palette, Screen shake and Menu sounds A/B. The sample experiment is a test plugin and is left out.
