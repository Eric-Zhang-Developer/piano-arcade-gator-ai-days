2. Hour-0 test

Raw LED 0 lights green near DIN, and the last LED lights green at the far end. If LED 0 shows red instead, the color order is wrong (the sketch says GRB).

3. Calibrate

Nudge until the lit LEDs are centered on the key. Steps are 1/4 LED (1.7 mm).

C8 (highest)
Joint gap Fix keys just right of the strip joint after A0 and C8 look right.
