## Report

**Setup.** Home rig: MSI monitor, long flat slider bar on the desk, left air tower (6 ring LEDs), right vertical blue LED strip, desk speakers, warm lamp light. One fixed camera behind-left of the player, slightly above, no cuts. The screen fills the right two-thirds of the frame and the slider runs across the bottom. Excitement comes from three light sources in one shot (screen, slider, towers) and white gloves blurring on air moves.

**Screen.** Perspective highway, narrow at the top. Notes vary in width: red taps, yellow and purple holds, light-blue slides, green air arrows. "COMBO" plus a very large white number sits mid-highway at about 40% height, with notes scrolling over it. Judgments (JUSTICE CRITICAL, JUSTICE, AIR) are small gold pops at combo height above the hit lane. Hits show a ring, a star burst and light columns at the judgment line. The top HUD shows judgment counts, score and a clear gauge. FULL COMBO is a large gold banner over a dimmed highway.

**Controller.** Idle cells are cyan/white and turn purple under the hands, even before any notes appear (0:16). The highway lane above the hands lights up at the same time. Some purple appears with no hand present, so the lights are partly game-driven. Not visible: tower LEDs reacting to air notes. Hand, light and lit lane in one shot tie the hands to the screen.

**Filming.** The single static wide shot works. Shooting from behind hides the combo under raised arms (3:01) and shrinks the judgment text. The take runs through the result screen (SSS), the payoff.

**Sound.** Integrated -29.9 LUFS, peak -11 dB, loudness range 4.8 LU: quiet and never normalized. L/R correlation is about 0.48, a faint 2 kHz tone shows in silent gaps, and an 8.3–9.5 kHz notch runs throughout. This is consistent with the camera mic recording the speakers, not a line feed. Transients run at roughly note rate, but I can't separate hit sounds from the drums. Loudness: -52 before the song, a flat -28 to -31 during play, -53 under FULL COMBO (3:03–3:06), loudest (-27) in the results music. The FC moment is nearly silent. Limits: numbers and spectrograms only, so sounds are unidentified.

**Steal.**
1. Light the pressed key's lane plus a judgment-line glow even with no note present.
2. A huge combo number mid-highway with notes drawn over it, so it needs no lane width.
3. Small judgment pops above the hit lane, with running counts in a top HUD.
4. A full-screen FULL COMBO banner, then a results screen. End the demo on it.
5. Demo: one static shot framing hands, keys and screen together, with game audio recorded as a line feed.

**Avoid.**
1. Filming from behind: hands cover the HUD. Shoot from above or beside the keys.
2. Judgment pops alone are too small on 88 lanes for teaching. Pair them with a color flash on the key.
3. Air notes and free-width notes have no piano equivalent.
