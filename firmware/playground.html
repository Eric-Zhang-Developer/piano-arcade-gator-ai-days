LED Playground

Play the strip with no piano: on-screen keys, your computer keyboard, colors and effects. Desktop Chrome only (Web Serial). Only one page can hold the board, so close the tester or the game's lights first.

not connected

Play

Click the keys, or use your computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play C to C. Z / X shift the octave (now C4).

Look

Pick one. Calm (the default) lights each key with the hint build-up, wrong-key red and soft glows for the count-in, fever, pause and the end. Demo adds the ripple: every hit breaks out both ways along the strip. The board's BOOT button and the game's pause menu switch it too.
