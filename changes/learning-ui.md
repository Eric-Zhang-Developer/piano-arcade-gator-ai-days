## What shipped
Current desktop learning frontend: guided mastery map, three-state parent/leaf examples, one prepared Start/real Resume, four existing activities and a coherent map-to-question handoff. Canonical product philosophy and a contributor design/asset handoff accompany the editable baseline.
On `/learn`: a map of music skills, from pitch and rhythm to phrase endings, with the next lesson ready to start. The ink is the tutor: activities tell it each judged note (right or wrong), each scored take (pass or fail) and each move on (next) through the `etude:ink` window event in `ink/cue.ts`. The theory lesson and the brand preview no longer show the gator; the lesson keeps an empty `data-tutor-perch` for the ink to rest on.
