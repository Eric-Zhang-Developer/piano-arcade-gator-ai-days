## What shipped
The 8 beats before the first note, drawn on the click's clock. In the listen half, 4 cells above the hit line fill one per beat, accented beats taller. In the count half, 4 3 2 1 in Saira, accented numerals in primary, while the cells drain. The hit line pulses on every beat and burns white for one frame on beat 0. Every beat time is a `beatHits` count-in click time on the same chart clock. Reduced motion cuts on the beat. There's no title slate: it repeated the HUD's song card (decision `count-in-slate`).
## Where it lives
`web/src/game/count-in/` (`countInAt`, the draw plugin, tests, `verify-count-in.mjs`), its line in `web/src/game/layers.ts`, the count-in click log in `web/src/game/beat/index.ts`, and `hw.countInBeat = null` in `web/src/app/play/page.tsx`. Decisions `count-in-look`, `count-in-slate`, `count-in-sync`. Shots and the before/after WebM are in `specs/features/count-in/shots/`.
## How to check it
`node web/src/game/count-in/verify-count-in.mjs --out <dir>`: not verified locally; covered by main's scheduled full run. Vitest `src/game/count-in src/game/beat`: 23 pass. In the dev server, stepped at 60 fps on Twinkle, every beat's first frame was 0–16.7 ms after its time, each click was scheduled at its beat's chart time, and a pause on beat 3 then resume left 0 stray numerals.
