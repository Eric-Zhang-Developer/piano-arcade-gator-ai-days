## What shipped
Adaptive chunks. "Practice in chunks" now starts on the whole song at 70% (`practice=adapt`) instead of walking it 4 bars at a time. The window then follows the player. Below 85%, it halves onto the half with more missed notes ("Smaller: bars 7–12"). After 2 clean takes, it grows ("Next: bars 1–8"). A fail after a grow steps back to the length that passed, so the search closes in. At one phrase, it offers "Learn bars …" in wait mode and "Slower: 50%". When the whole song passes clean, it's done. The units are weak-bars' 2-bar phrases, of the played hand only. The start and results screens show a strip with one cell per phrase. The window is lit, and after a take each cell is tinted from gold to miss by its accuracy. The fixed walk (`practice=1`) still works by URL. Shots: `specs/features/adaptive-chunks/shots/`.
