# Mood frames

Frames the team generated itself (Codex image generation) or captured from our own app, so they can be committed,
unlike the commercial-game frames under `../references/`, which decision 003 keeps local.

They're moods, not data: an image model drew the notation, levels and counts, so never copy a note, level or count from
them. They're not a decision either: `../visual-direction.md` is the look, and a change to it goes through a `contract:` PR.

- `codex-round-1/`: three directions for the music select, plus the playfield and results in one of them, and the
  current select as the baseline.
- `codex-classical/`: classical paper across five screens. Eric rejected it as the app's look ("we lose the whole
  arcade heritage of the game"), but kept it for parts.
