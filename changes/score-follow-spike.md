## What shipped
A prototype spike (#519): `?mode=flow&band=follow` plays a bass-and-drums band (channels 2 and 10) on a clock that predicts your next note; `&band=hold` plays the same band on flow mode's clock for an A/B. Any other URL sends nothing. Findings: the predicting clock is 10× closer than flow's clock (medians 0–30 ms vs 147–363 ms) but misses the spike's bar on the jittered drift player (Twinkle 30/72 ms median/p95, Für Elise 18/58; the bar is 25/50). Recommendation: iterate. The second box (a Kalman follower on phase, tempo and its trend, now `band=follow`) clears the bar on Für Elise (15/33) and Twinkle's median (15), and misses Twinkle's p95 (63); recommendation: Eric's listen, no more estimator boxes.
