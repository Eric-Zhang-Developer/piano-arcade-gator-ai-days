## What shipped
The sheet beams eighths and sixteenths the way a score does: by the quarter, or by the dotted quarter in 3/8, 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8 (`beamGroups`, `shouldBeam`). sheet-xml writes them as `<beam>` elements and sheet-view turns OSMD's `autoBeam` off, because `autoBeam` splits runs at every eighth in n/8. A beam takes its notes' judgment color once all of them are judged and agree, and stays ink until then. Für Elise before and after: `specs/features/sheet-beams/shots/`.
## Where it lives
`web/src/game/sheet-beams/` (`index.ts`, tests, `verify-beams.mjs`); the `<beam>` writer in `web/src/game/sheet-xml/index.ts` (#531); the measure and paint in `web/src/game/sheet-view/follow.ts` and `SheetView.tsx` (#536).
