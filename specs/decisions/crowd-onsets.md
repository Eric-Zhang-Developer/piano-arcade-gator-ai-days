# crowd: how a clap is told from the piano

Feature `crowd` (#1345, PR #1361). It records the research, the fixtures, the measured results and every setting in
`crowdSettings` (`web/src/game/x/crowd/dsp.ts`).

## What the research says
A research subagent read these sources; its findings are summarized here, and it flagged what it couldn't confirm.
- **A clap is a short noise burst through a low-Q resonance.**
  - It lasts about 5 ms anechoic. Its cavity resonance sits at 0.5–1.6 kHz, by hand configuration, "always … well
    below 2000 Hz" (Jylhä & Erkut, DAFx-08, Table 1).
  - It decays within about 10 ms. Flat, matching palms add a broadband, non-resonant edge (Fu et al., Phys. Rev.
    Research 7, 013259, 2025).
  - No source measures a clap's spectral flatness.
- **A piano onset fires spectral flux too.**
  - Flux finds piano onsets at P 0.958 and R 0.969 (Dixon, DAFx-06).
  - Hammer contact lasts about 1–4 ms (Askenfelt & Jansson, 1990), and hammer attacks look "drum like"
    (Barry et al., ISSC 2005).
  - So the piano is the main source of false claps, and a gate on how noise-like the frame is does real work.
- **Thresholds and online peak picking:**
  - a moving mean or median plus δ (Bello et al. 2005);
  - log(1 + λ|X|) compression, a causal mean, a local maximum over the last frames, and a minimum gap between onsets
    (Böck, Krebs & Schedl, ISMIR 2012).
- **Spectral flatness** is the geometric over the arithmetic mean of power: 0 for a tone, and about 0.56 for a single
  frame of white noise (the subagent's own derivation, not a source), so a gate near 1 never fires.
- **Median-filter HPSS** (FitzGerald, DAFx-10) needs future frames to separate in time, so it isn't used.
- **The mic:** the W3C spec lets a page turn echo cancellation, noise suppression and AGC off "so that the audio is not
  altered". The browsers' current defaults weren't confirmed.

## The design that follows
- **Flux of the 2–8 kHz band.** It catches the clap's broadband edge, where the piano's partials are weak; the clap's
  resonance is below the band on purpose. Log compression is `log(1 + 1000|X|)`.
- **The window and hop: 512 and 128** at 48 kHz (10.7 ms and 2.7 ms).
  - The research suggested 1024 and 256. 512 is about one clap long, so a burst isn't smeared across a long window.
  - A 128 hop is one AudioWorklet render quantum: a frame per block, with no buffering across blocks.
- **The threshold:** λ × the mean of the last 150 ms of flux + δ. The peak is called one frame late, once the next
  frame is lower.
- **The flatness gate:** the larger of the peak frame's flatness and the next frame's.
  - The peak frame holds only the burst's first hop against a window of piano: one clap read 0.223 there, under the
    gate.
  - A band whose mean bin power is under `floor` reads 0, since digital silence otherwise reads 1.
- **Near a MIDI note-on** (±30 ms), the threshold is ×1.5, never blanked. The note-on is posted to the worklet on the
  AudioContext clock.
- **The minimum gap is 60 ms.** The research gives 30 ms against double triggers (Böck) and an unsourced 80–100 ms for
  crowds.
  - In the sweep, 40 ms reads a little better on the stress bed: R 0.979 against 0.958.
  - 60 was kept because a room's claps on one beat smear over a few tens of ms, which the fixtures (one clap per beat)
    can't show. Eric's listen (spec d) is the check.
- **The mic constraints:** `echoCancellation`, `noiseSuppression` and `autoGainControl` are all false (`crowdUi`), since
  AGC pumping and noise suppression can eat claps. `getSettings().latency`, when the browser reports it, is taken off
  each clap's time.
- **`offsetMs: -2.5`:** a frame's flux peak to the clap's first sample, the fixtures' signed median, so 0 on them.

## Fixtures
- **No downloads.** Claps are synthesized (`mix.ts`, `clapSettings`): seeded noise through an RBJ band-pass at
  0.5–1.6 kHz with Q 2–5, plus a broadband edge at 0.35, a 0.5 ms attack and a 2.5 ms decay constant.
  - Each clap is jittered around its beat (a soft bell, ±45 ms at most).
- **The bed** is the laptop voice (`laptop-sound/synth.ts`) ported to plain arithmetic: a triangle plus a sine an octave
  up through a lowpass that opens with velocity. It plays twinkle with 0.5 s of silence first.
- **The stress bed** adds a 1.5 ms high-passed noise burst at every strike: the hammer a real piano has and the laptop
  voice doesn't (Askenfelt & Jansson). It's reported, not gated.
- **The level** is each clap's RMS over its first 20 ms against the bed's RMS over the span of the notes.
- **The match window:** a found onset counts if it's within ±30 ms of a true clap, each matched once. That's tighter
  than the papers' 50 ms and near Böck's ±25 ms.
- `make-fixtures.mjs` writes the first 4 s of each level as WAVs, with the true clap times beside them, into
  `fixtures/`. The WAVs stay out of git; vitest builds the same mixes in memory, so CI needs no WAV.

## Results (`pnpm -C web exec vitest run src/game/x/crowd`)
Each result is the worst of 3 seeds, over 48 claps (one per beat):

| Bed | Level | Precision | Recall | Median \|error\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| laptop voice | 0 dB | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.7 ms |
| laptop voice | −6 dB | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.7 ms |
| laptop voice | −12 dB | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.7 ms |
| stress (hammer) | −6 dB | 0.958 | 0.958 | |

- **The stress bed's limit:** a hammer burst landing 30–35 ms before a clap takes the onset. The clap then falls inside
  the minimum gap, and the burst is 30 ms or more from the clap, so it misses the match window.
- **The bar** (spec Validation a: at −6 dB, P and R ≥ 0.9 and median |error| ≤ 15 ms) passes on the laptop bed, and on
  the stress bed too.
- **What the fixtures don't cover:** real claps, a real room and the DGX's acoustic sound. They're synthesized to the
  research's numbers, so they are easier than a room is likely to be.

## CPU (spec Validation b)
- **What was measured:** `ClapDetector.push` per 128-sample block, offline in node: the worklet's own text, but not
  inside an AudioWorklet. Three runs; the budget is 2.67 ms.
  - Quiet machine: the mean was 0.65–0.77% of the budget, and p99 was 2.0–3.8%.
  - A loaded run (other agents' builds) had a mean of 5.0–6.4% and p99 of 32–65%.
- **The bar** is a mean under 30%. It held in both runs.

## The sweep
- `CROWD_SWEEP=1 pnpm -C web exec vitest run src/game/x/crowd/sweep.test.ts` scores λ, δ, flatMin, minGapMs and
  noteRaise over 243 settings at −6 dB. It takes about 7 minutes.
- All 243 settings clear the bar on the laptop bed.
- Only δ = 8 gives P = R = 1 on it (81 settings). δ 2 and 4 let the bed's end click through (P 0.98).
- Over that range the stress bed moves only with minGapMs (40 is better, as above). λ, flatMin and noteRaise don't
  change it.
- **Chosen:** λ 1.6 and flatMin 0.25, the middle of their flat ranges; δ 8; minGapMs 60; noteRaise 1.5, between 1 and
  2, which read the same.
