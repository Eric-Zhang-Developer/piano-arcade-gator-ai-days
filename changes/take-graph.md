## What shipped
A results panel that plots every note's timing over the song: one dot per note at its offset (late up, early down), in its hand's color from hand-colors and shaped by hand (circle right, square left), over shaded PERFECT (±25 ms) and GREAT (±50 ms) bands and dashed GOOD (±100 ms) lines. A MISS is an × on the zero line and a WRONG note a red tick at the top. A faint velocity line shows on a right-hand axis when the input senses velocity (MIDI, test, bots); laptop takes say they have none. Each dot names "Bar 5, beat 2 · +32 ms · GREAT · velocity 74" as its tooltip and accessible name, and "Show as table" lists the same rows. Plain SVG, 900 px wide, 230 px tall.
## Where it lives
`web/src/game/take-graph/` (`graph.ts` pure points, `TakeGraph.tsx` the panel, `index.ts` the plugin, `graph.test.ts`, `verify-take-graph.mjs`), one entry in `web/src/game/layers.ts`, screenshots in `specs/features/take-graph/screens/`.
