## Report

**Layout.** One 3D highway, five lanes (green, red, yellow, blue, orange), centered and filling the bottom ~55% of the screen. Strong perspective: it narrows to about a third of its width at the horizon. Faint horizontal beat lines cross it. Strike line = five colored ring "frets" at the bottom. HUD sits *beside* the highway, not on it: left is a score box (six-digit score, x1–x4 multiplier dial with a tier color: x2 orange, x3 green, x4 purple, x8 blue in star power), a column of pips for progress to the next tier, and a streak odometer. Right is the rock meter gauge (red/yellow/green arc, needle) with six star-power tubes above it. The 3D stage, band and crowd fill everything else, and the camera cuts constantly.

**Notes.** Round domed gems in lane color. Two cap styles appear: glowing white caps (0m20s) versus flat grey caps (1m30s run). I can't confirm from the footage which is the hammer-on. Sustains are solid neon beams. Star-phrase notes get a cyan star outline. Chords are just gems side by side.

**Feedback.** Hit: a flame on that fret for ~0.2–0.3 s, kept inside the lane. Held sustain: white sparks at the fret. Streak milestones every 100 (first at 50): text pops in above the highway, swells, then shrinks out over ~2.5 s. Star-phrase complete: star sparkles fly to the meter, then "Star Power Ready". Star power: lightning edges, all notes cyan, x8, scene over-exposed for ~7 s. Miss: not visible (full combo). Ending: "You Rock!" flame pillar.

**Motion and timing.** A note takes about 0.8 s from the horizon to the strike line on Expert (measured at 10 fps around 7:21). Dense runs read as ladders because gems are evenly spaced and identical.

**Palette and mood.** Dark purple/black stage, neon green and magenta set pieces. The highway itself stays dark and low-contrast, so the saturated gems and orange flames carry the energy. Star power is the one moment that floods the whole screen.

**Sound.** Mono 22 kHz extract, read from EBU R128 loudness and spectrograms only. I can't hear it. The song body is flat and loud: momentary −19 to −25 LUFS for most of the run, with dips to about −29/−30 at ~0:45 and ~3:45. Before the song, four evenly spaced broadband clicks (~3.0–5.8 s) line up with the empty highway, which looks like a count-in. Hit sounds: no transient separate from the drums is visible. Every note was hit, so there are no miss sounds to see. At star power activation (1:34.0), loudness rises ~4.5 LU over 1 s (−23.2 → −18.6), but a song section change happens at the same time, so I can't tell whether that's a game sound. A rising pitch sweep at ~1:40.5–1:41.8 overlaps a held star-phrase chord; it fits a whammy bend, but that's unconfirmed. After "You Rock!" (7:25.3) there is a broadband burst, then ~13 s of diffuse noise with faint whistle-like curves. That looks like crowd cheering, and it's the clearest game-added audio. The limits: no stem separation, no transcription, and I couldn't attribute anything that overlaps the music.

**Steal.**
1. Keep HUD off the playfield: score/multiplier/streak cluster on one side, health gauge on the other, so thin piano lanes stay clean.
2. Tier color on the multiplier (orange/green/purple/blue) plus a pip bar to the next tier. It reads at a glance without numbers.
3. Hit feedback sized to the lane (a flame/flash on just that key), with a separate spark stream for held notes.
4. Milestone text in the space above the falling notes, animated in and out over ~2.5 s.
5. One rare whole-screen state (star power) as the big payoff, keeping everything else lane-local.

**Avoid.**
1. Constant camera cuts and a busy 3D stage behind a highway this small. On 88 keys the notes need the screen.
2. Strong 3D perspective: it squeezes far lanes, and 88 lanes would turn to slivers. Synthesia's flat, key-aligned layout fits.
3. Recoloring every note during the bonus mode (all-cyan). A learner loses the hand/pitch color cue right when it matters.
