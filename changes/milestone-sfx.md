## What shipped
Layered milestone sounds, never per hit: combo steps (climbing with the count), fever start (a riser that lands on the flash's peak), the clear card's CLEAR / FULL COMBO / ALL PERFECT, the rank hit, the full-combo fanfare and NEW RECORD at its stamp. Each voice is a few synthesized layers (body, shimmer, noise). Under a recording, a sound landing on a loud stretch (above −16 dBFS, measured) plays on time at −12 dB. Since #1396: FULL COMBO and ALL PERFECT follow NOSTALGIA's measured sequence (a click, five music-box tones, a tremolo chord) with no drumroll under it; a whoosh on Start, a deflate when a miss ends a combo of 20 or more, a deeper rank thud, a pop as the gator tutor appears and a settle chord on the results; one bus and a compressor under the DGX; and /lab/sounds, every milestone sound on one page, one button each and Play all, through the game's mix and your Effects volume. Mute, the effects volume, the miss cue and wait mode work as before; juice-sound's scheduler is still the one place sounds are scheduled.
## Where it lives
`web/src/game/milestone-sfx/` (voices, clear and NEW RECORD timing, loudness rule, tests), `web/src/game/sound/index.ts` (`scheduler`), `web/src/game/audio-track/index.ts` (`recordingLevelAt`), `specs/decisions/milestone-sfx-loudness.md`, `milestone-sfx-mix.md` and `milestone-sfx-mix-pass.md`; the board in `web/src/game/milestone-sfx/Board.tsx` and `web/src/app/lab/sounds/`.
